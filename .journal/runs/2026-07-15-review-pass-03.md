# Review Pass 03 — Provenance and Resume

## Trace

- Dataset generation derives deterministic-looking per-slot seeds and shifts them
  by a fixed retry offset.
- The worker passes a seed to `cli.generate_scenario`.
- The scenario writer writes only generated CBS YAML structures.
- Runtime evaluation writes `run_metrics.json`.
- Resume skips a slot based on `is_solved=true` alone.

## Conclusion

Seed arithmetic exists during execution but is not durable sample provenance.
Validation verdicts are not fingerprinted to the inputs or validator. This can
mix old and new contracts in one resumed dataset and prevents reliable forensic
replay.
